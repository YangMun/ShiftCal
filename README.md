# ShiftCal

Free online shift schedule calculators for US shift workers — nurses, firefighters, police, and plant operators.

Enter your rotation pattern (2-2-3 Panama, Pitman, DuPont, 4-on-4-off, 48/96, Kelly, or a custom cycle) and your team's anchor date, and ShiftCal builds your personalized 12-month work calendar — with US holidays and paydays overlaid — exportable as an ICS file straight into your phone's calendar.

Built as a fast, static site. No account, no install, works offline once loaded.
